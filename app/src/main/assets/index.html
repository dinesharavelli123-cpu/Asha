🧩 Why it matters

A single low day is not the same as a sustained change. ASHA combines mood, sleep, social connection and language tone to avoid overreacting to one signal.

🔒 Privacy-first demo

This prototype keeps its scoring logic on-device. A production version could use consent-based cloud AI while keeping sensitive data minimised.

🤝 Human-in-the-loop

When risk grows, the product concept prioritises trusted contacts and trained human support rather than pretending AI can replace professional care.
